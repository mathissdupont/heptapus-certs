# Hetzner account identity and KVKK transfer request

Status: owner action draft, 2026-09-24. **Not sent.** This is an operational request template, not legal advice and not a signed agreement.

## Why this is needed

The Hetzner customer account and generated GDPR Data Processing Agreement currently name only **Heptapus Group**. Heptapus Group is presently a brand/trading name, not an incorporated legal entity. HeptaCert is operated by **Samet Ünsal**.

Hetzner's terms require customer account data to be correct and complete. Its DPA identifies the account's contract partner as the controller/client. The account identity therefore needs to be corrected or formally confirmed before HeptaCert relies on that DPA in public legal text.

Separately, continuous hosting of Turkish users' personal data in Helsinki is an international transfer under Turkish Law No. 6698. The existing GDPR DPA documents processor duties, but it must not be assumed to replace the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority's Standard Contract No. 2 (controller to processor). The Authority says the published standard wording must not be altered, except for its selectable provisions, and a signed contract must be notified within five business days.

## Request 1 — correct the customer identity

Send this through the authenticated Hetzner support account. Do not include passwords, API tokens or server credentials.

**Subject:** Correction of customer/contract partner name for an individual operating under a brand

> Hello,
>
> My Hetzner account and the Data Processing Agreement currently identify the customer as “Heptapus Group”. Heptapus Group is a brand/trading name and is not an incorporated company. I, Samet Ünsal, am the individual account holder and operator of the HeptaCert service under that brand.
>
> Please tell me how to correct the contractual customer name and DPA party so that the legal customer is recorded as “Samet Ünsal”, while “Heptapus Group” may remain only as the trading/brand name if your system supports it. Please also tell me which identity or address evidence you require and whether a new DPA must be generated after the correction.
>
> Please do not change, cancel or transfer any products as part of this request without confirming the exact action with me first.
>
> Kind regards,
> Samet Ünsal
> contact@heptapusgroup.com

Do not invent a postal address. If Hetzner requires proof or a service address, provide only a current, truthful address that Samet is legally entitled to use. Keep the ticket response and the regenerated account/DPA documents with the compliance records.

## Request 2 — ask whether Hetzner will execute the Turkish standard contract

Send this only after Hetzner confirms the account-party identity. Attach the Authority's unmodified **Standard Contract No. 2 — Controller to Processor** and the completed appendices when they are ready.

**Subject:** Turkish Law No. 6698 Standard Contract No. 2 for Helsinki hosting

> Hello,
>
> I operate the HeptaCert service as the data controller and use Hetzner Online GmbH as the processor for production hosting and backups in Helsinki, Finland. We already have Hetzner's GDPR Data Processing Agreement.
>
> Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 requires an appropriate safeguard for this continuous international transfer. The Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority publishes Standard Contract No. 2 for transfers from a controller to a processor.
>
> Does Hetzner Online GmbH have a process for reviewing and executing the Authority's unmodified Standard Contract No. 2 and its completed appendices? If yes, please route this request to the person authorized to sign for Hetzner and tell me the accepted signature method. If Hetzner cannot execute this contract, please confirm that in writing so I can evaluate another lawful transfer mechanism or hosting arrangement.
>
> This request does not ask to replace or amend Hetzner's existing GDPR DPA.
>
> Kind regards,
> Samet Ünsal
> contact@heptapusgroup.com

## Facts for the contract appendices — draft only

These facts are owner-confirmed but must be reconciled with the live system and the Authority's current template before signature:

- Data exporter/controller: Samet Ünsal, operating HeptaCert under the Heptapus Group brand.
- Data importer/processor: Hetzner Online GmbH.
- Processing locations: Helsinki production infrastructure and Helsinki backups.
- Data-subject groups: organization administrators/staff, event attendees, certificate recipients, public members, speakers and support contacts where applicable.
- Ordinary data categories: identity/contact data, account and organization data, event registration answers, attendance/check-in records, certificate records, session data, support messages, authentication/security logs and uploaded event materials.
- Special-category data: not a general platform requirement. If an organizer has a genuine event-specific need, the collection must have a separately documented purpose and legal basis, restricted access, a defined retention/anonymization rule and exclusion from general integrations by default.
- Processing purposes: host, secure, back up and make the HeptaCert service available on the controller's documented instructions.
- Retention: organizer-configured event retention and anonymization; account-deletion cleanup intended within 30 days unless a documented legal exception applies. Do not insert unsupported fixed periods.
- Subprocessors, security measures and deletion/return obligations: copy from the current Hetzner DPA/TOM and subprocessor disclosures after verifying their latest versions.

## Decision gate

Do not publish a statement that the Helsinki transfer is “fully KVKK compliant” merely because the GDPR DPA exists.

- If Hetzner corrects the party identity and signs the Authority's exact Standard Contract No. 2, complete the required notification within five business days and retain proof.
- If Hetzner corrects the identity but refuses the Turkish standard contract, record the refusal and assess another Article 9 safeguard or a different hosting arrangement before claiming compliance.
- If Hetzner cannot recognize Samet Ünsal as the actual contract party, resolve the account ownership issue before relying on the DPA.
